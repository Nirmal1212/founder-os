# Channel & distribution — one piece, many surfaces

Production is expensive; distribution is leverage. The highest-ROI move is making one strong asset travel across channels in each channel's native form.

## Repurpose: one → many
A single substantial piece (a researched blog post, a teardown, a data study) becomes:
- A **LinkedIn/X thread** with the core insight and a hook.
- A **newsletter** section or whole issue.
- 3–5 **short social posts** — one per sub-insight, each standalone.
- A **community** post/comment where the topic is being discussed.
- A short **video/clip** or carousel if the format fits.
Plan this at creation time, not as an afterthought.

## Adapt, don't copy-paste
Each channel has norms; identical cross-posting underperforms everywhere:
- **LinkedIn**: professional insight, a strong first line (the hook before "see more"), short paragraphs, a takeaway.
- **X/Threads**: punchy, opinionated, the hook in the first post; threads for depth.
- **Newsletter**: a personal voice, one clear idea per issue, a single CTA.
- **Communities (Reddit, Slack, Discord, niche forums)**: contribute value first; be a member, not a billboard — self-promo without contribution gets ignored or banned.

## Hooks earn the read
The first line/frame decides whether anything below is seen. Lead with the tension, the surprising result, or the reader's problem — not "I wrote a blog post about…". Spend real effort on the hook; it's the highest-leverage sentence.

## Owned > rented
Rented audiences (social algorithms) are powerful but borrowed and volatile. Use them to drive to **owned** channels (email list, community) you control. The newsletter list is the compounding asset; social is the top of that funnel.

## Cadence and consistency
Show up reliably on the chosen channels. Algorithms and audiences reward consistency; a sporadic presence never builds momentum. Better to own two channels consistently than six occasionally.

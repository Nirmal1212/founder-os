# Channel playbooks — tactics & culture per channel

Each launch channel has its own norms; what wins on one gets you buried on another. Respect the culture or don't bother.

## Product Hunt
- **Rally your network** ahead of time (don't ask for "upvotes" directly — against the rules; ask people to "check it out").
- Launch 12:01am PT; **be present all day** replying to every comment — engagement drives ranking.
- Strong assets: clear tagline, gallery, a founder first-comment telling the story, a special offer for the community.
- **Never buy votes or use sockpuppets** — PH detects it, penalizes, and the reputational hit outlasts the spike.

## Hacker News (Show HN)
- Authentic, technical, humble. HN punishes marketing tone instantly.
- Lead with what you built and the interesting technical/problem angle; founders should respond substantively.
- Fragile: you can't orchestrate it, vote-rings get flagged, and a defensive founder tanks the thread. Post and engage honestly; accept you don't control it.

## PR / press
- Journalists need an **angle they care about**, not your feature — a trend, a notable number, a conflict, a "why now."
- Build a simple press kit (story, facts, assets, quotes, contact). Personalize pitches to the specific reporter's beat.
- Exclusives/embargoes can land bigger coverage — offer one outlet first, embargo until launch.
- Small startups rarely get press from product alone; tie to a narrative or data.

## Communities (Reddit, Slack/Discord, niche forums)
- **Give value first.** Be a real member before you post; most communities ban drive-by promotion.
- Share where self-promo is allowed/welcomed, framed as helping not selling.
- The right niche community of true ICP beats a big generic one.

## Email (your owned channel — highest converting)
- The waitlist/customer list is the most reliable launch lever — you own it, it converts best.
- Segment (waitlist vs. existing users vs. churned) and tailor the ask.
- Clear single CTA. Drafting handed to `nirmal-ai-content`.

## Social (founder voice)
- Lead with the **narrative** (from positioning), not a dry feature list. A story/hook outperforms an announcement.
- Founder accounts usually outperform brand accounts early. Thread the why, not just the what.
- Repurpose the one launch story into channel-native posts (handled with `nirmal-ai-content`).

## Sequencing
- Tier-1: coordinate a same-day multi-channel moment.
- Tier-2/3: stagger — email + social day one, community/PH when it fits — to sustain attention rather than blow it in an hour.

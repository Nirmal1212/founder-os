## Warm-up (5 min)
- Tell me about your role and a typical day.
- Where does <problem area> show up in that?

## The last time (20 min)
- Tell me about the **last time** you <faced the problem>. Walk me through it.
- What were you trying to accomplish?
- What did you try? What was hard?
- What did you do instead / as a workaround?
- *(probe each answer: "why was that?" / "what do you mean by…?")*

## Magnitude (5 min)
- How often does this happen?
- What does it cost you (time / money / stress) when it does?
- Have you tried to solve it before? Paid for anything?

## Open (5 min)
- What didn't I ask about that I should have?
- Who else should I talk to?

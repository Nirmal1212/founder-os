# Sales pitch deck — <Product Name>

> Sales deck (ask = trial/pilot/purchase). Keep separate from the investor deck. All copy pulls from the messaging house.

| # | Slide | Content | Goal |
|---|-------|---------|------|
| 1 | Title / hook | One-liner + a line that frames the shift | Set the frame |
| 2 | The problem | The buyer's pain, made vivid + "why now" | They nod |
| 3 | Cost of the problem | What status quo costs them | Create urgency |
| 4 | The solution | Product as the answer — outcome, not features | The reveal |
| 5 | How it works | 3 value pillars, each → benefit | Make it concrete |
| 6 | Proof | Metrics, customers, testimonials (truthful) | Convert skeptics |
| 7 | Why us / why now | Unique insight / unfair advantage | Defensibility |
| 8 | Pricing (value-anchored) | Tie to ROI; benchmark-aware | Frame the value |
| 9 | The ask | One clear next step (pilot/trial/deal) | Move forward |

**Notes**
- Lead with their world (slide 2), not your company.
- Replace feature bullets with outcomes / before-after.
- Cut any slide that doesn't advance problem → solution → proof → ask.
- For the actual .pptx, follow the pptx skill.

## Positioning canvas
| Element | Answer |

| Target | Heads of CS (P-001) at 50–200 person SaaS companies (D-001) |
| Competitive alternatives | Spreadsheets, CRM health fields, enterprise CS platforms |
| Unique attributes | Joins CRM, billing and usage data; explains every score change; live in a day |
| Value | See churn risk 30+ days before renewal and know why |
| Category | Customer health monitoring (not "full CS platform") |

## Messaging house
- **One-liner:** Northwind Pulse tells your CS team which accounts are about to churn, and why, before the renewal call.
- **Pillar 1: Early.** Risk Alerts (T-004) arrive weeks before renewal. *Proof:* alert lead-time metric from pilot customers.
- **Pillar 2: Explained.** Every Health Score (T-003) shows the Signals (T-002) behind it. *Proof:* the "why" panel.
- **Pillar 3: Fast to value.** Three integrations, running in a day. *Proof:* median setup time.
- **POV:** You don't need an enterprise CS suite to stop surprise churn.

Proof points are targets until pilots finish. Do not publish them as results.

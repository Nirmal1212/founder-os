# Research synthesis — churn visibility
<!-- Produced by: founder-os-research (Mode B + C). Read context v1; wrote back P-001, P-002, D-001. ILLUSTRATIVE: interviews are fictional. -->

**Method:** 8 customer-discovery interviews (Heads of CS and CS Ops, 50–200 person SaaS). Confidence: medium (n=8, one segment, recruited via warm intros).

## Themes
| # | Theme | Evidence | Confidence |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Churn is discovered at renewal, not before | 6 of 8 recalled a surprise churn in the last year | High |
| 2 | Health data lives in 3–5 tools nobody joins | 7 of 8 keep a manual spreadsheet (P-002 work) | High |
| 3 | CSMs ignore scores they don't trust | 5 of 8 abandoned a prior scoring attempt | Medium |
| 4 | Enterprise CS platforms are priced out of reach | 6 of 8 cited a quote above budget | Medium |

## Jobs-to-be-done

- When Monday starts, I want one view of account health so I don't rebuild it by hand. (P-002)

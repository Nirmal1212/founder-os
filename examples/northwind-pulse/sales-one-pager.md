## Stop finding out about churn at renewal
Northwind Pulse joins your HubSpot, Stripe and product data into one Health Score per account, and alerts your team in Slack when an account is slipping.

**Who it's for:** Heads of Customer Success (P-001) and CS Ops (P-002) at 50–200 person SaaS companies.

| You have today | With Pulse |
|---|---|
| A Monday spreadsheet | A daily-updated, ranked health list |
| Surprise churn at renewal | Risk Alerts weeks earlier |
| Scores nobody trusts | A "why" panel for every change |

**How it works:** 1. Connect HubSpot, Stripe, Segment. 2. We score every account daily. 3. Your team gets alerts and logs the action.

**Pricing:** flat monthly tiers by account count, designed to stay far below enterprise CS suites (C-004).

**Discovery questions for the first call**
- How did you find out about your last churned customer?
- Where does account health live today, and who maintains it?
- What would you do with a 30-day warning?

**Objection: "We already have health fields in our CRM."** One source shows usage OR billing. Pulse combines them and explains the score.
